Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

This policy explains what information the Trimly app ("the App") collects, how it's used, and who can see it. Trimly is a salon management app used by salon owners and their staff to manage bookings, billing, inventory, and payroll.

Who this policy covers

The App is used directly by salon owners and their employees (collectively, "Users") — it is not a consumer-facing app for salon customers. A salon's customers never install the App or create an account in it; their contact and visit information is entered by salon staff as part of normal business record-keeping, described below.

Information we collect

DataFrom whomPurpose
Name, phone number, email address Employees & owners Account login, staff identification on bills and schedules
Role, base salary, commission rates, salary/commission payment records Employees & owners Payroll calculation and commission tracking
Attendance records (clock-in/out times) Employees Attendance tracking, payroll deductions
Name, phone number, email address, visit history, amount spent Entered by salon staff about their customers Billing, customer relationship management (e.g. VIP status, repeat-visit tracking)
Bills, payment method, service/product purchase details Generated by salon staff during checkout Point-of-sale record-keeping, revenue reporting

The App does not collect: precise location, camera/photo access, contacts, or advertising identifiers — it requests none of these device permissions.

Where data is stored

Each salon's data is stored in its own separate, isolated Google Firebase project (Firestore database and Firebase Authentication), hosted on Google Cloud infrastructure. One salon's data is never accessible from another salon's account or app session. A small, separate directory service is used only to route a login attempt to the correct salon's project by email address — it never stores passwords.

Who can see what

  • A salon owner can see all data belonging to their own salon: every employee's profile, pay details, customer records, and bills.
  • An employee can see customer records, bills, and the shared service/inventory catalog needed to do their job, but only their own pay/commission details — not a coworker's.
  • No salon's data is visible to any other salon, and the app's operator does not access an individual salon's business data as part of normal operation.
