Brief (owner, 2026-10-31): answers four jobs - how this month is going, real profit, who and what earns, clients to win back - plus stock. Current page is too scattered, numbers disagree, charts are hard to read, unpolished. One month at a time vs the month before.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Reports is a monthly statement with five sections, each answering one question in one sentence before any chart. Refuses the tab-strip-of-twenty-cards dashboard, and any figure summed from the capped bill list.

OWN-WORLD: The app's own: white DsCards on bgSurface, navy for the selected section and the single chart series, kChartCategorical only where categories are compared, status colours only for loss / overdue. Tabular figures.

STORY: Pick a month; read the answer at the top of each section (takings vs last month, profit after costs, top earner, clients to call, stock to reorder); go deeper only if needed.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Month stepper (prev / month / next). Laptop: section index left (Month, Profit, Team & services, Clients, Stock) with each section's one-line answer under its name; selected section right. Phone: same stepper, a chip row of the five sections, then the selected section.

FORM: Section index + page (position 7 of 7 drafted structures, dealt lead). seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
