# Owner > WhatsApp messages

Mode: Operate. Inherits the app's established world (navy on slate, flat, DsCard family; PRODUCT.md, lib/theme.dart, lib/widgets/ds.dart).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Each automatic message is a job the owner hires and checks on. The page is three task cards (Bill receipt, Birthday wish, Come-back reminder), each with its own switch, its rule in words and its count this month; tapping one opens that task's page. Refuses the settings-form-with-a-chart-bolted-on layout.

OWN-WORLD: The app's own: white DsCards on bgSurface, navy for switches and the current selection, slate text, WhatsApp green only on the WhatsApp mark. Sent/failed use the status colours (muted green / red), nothing else coloured.

STORY: The owner sees at a glance what goes out, when, and that it's working; changes a rule (reminder days, birthday timing) in one tap; checks a failure and why.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header with master switch and "from your WhatsApp number, 10:00 AM daily"; this month's total line; three task cards stacked, each one switch + one rule line + one count; setup checks (test send, run now) below.

FORM: Task cards -> task detail (position 4 of 7 drafted structures, dealt lead). seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
